# Revised guarantees and invariants

Contract 2.2.0,2026-11-16. These are normative **design** guarantees for implementation and re-review, conditional on the selected deployment/authority model. They are not claims of implemented security. Definitions in [C01–C16](../rag-data-contracts.md) and [S05](../rag-security-design.md#s05-revocation-publication-and-response-release) are shared across the package.

| I27 / RR05 | Loading/prewarming a new encoder cannot hold the mutation gate. | Prewarm outside gate; short gated readiness/membership/head CAS. Expiry remains I06 final-admission semantics with suspension limit and pending A09 acceptance. |

Lock order for every visible mutation: singleton gate → tenant serving/semantic/lifecycle heads → families → documents (UUID order in each tier) → job/target rows. Worker checkpoint transactions never call the authority while holding locks. Physical OCR/GC deletion/model build runs outside the gate under fenced immutable manifests. Outbox is notification/rebuild scheduling, never the source of live deny/semantic safety.

The selected model excludes multi-process content serving, automatic failover/rolling overlap, arbitrary SQL policy writers, automatic legal precedence, semantic-derived vector/sketch serving, fine-grained/redacted-only audiences and free synthesis. Those are explicit scope restrictions; adding them reopens the relevant contracts/approval/tests. [Remaining decisions](remaining-decisions.md) records who must accept the envelope. [Verification matrix](verification-matrix.md) lists deterministic tests without claiming future runtime passes.
