# Phases and quality gates

These gates preserve the specification's P00–P09 order and P10 optional branch. Entry/exit refer to **acceptance**,not a ban on drafting independent code/tests. A benchmark cannot be required before building the harness that produces it. Approval and execution evidence are separate. No gate has passed in this planning task.
